Donations of molecular biology laboratory supplies from the Philippine Genome Center (PGC) – DNA Sequencing Core Facility

The Philippine Science High School – Calabarzon Region Campus in Batangas City and the Philippine Science High School – Mimaropa Region Campus in Romblon received donations of molecular biology laboratory supplies from the Philippine Genome Center (PGC) – DNA Sequencing Core Facility in June 2026. 🧬🔬🇵🇭 The donated laboratory consumables—including filter tips, microcentrifuge tubes, autoclave bags, reagent reservoirs for multichannel pipettes, 96-well plates, and plate sealing films—will support the school’s laboratory activities and research initiatives in molecular biology and related fields. These resources will provide Pisay scholars with enhanced opportunities for hands-on learning and scientific inquiry, further strengthening the delivery of quality STEM education. The DOST-PSHS System expresses its sincere gratitude to the Philippine Genome Center for its generosity and continued partnership in advancing science education and research. As the country’s premier provider of quality STEM education at the secondary level, the Philippine Science High School System welcomes donations, partnerships, and collaborative initiatives from institutions, organizations, and individuals who share its commitment to nurturing the nation’s gifted young scholars. What will the Philippine Science High School System look like 30 years from now?

To answer this question, leaders and key personnel of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST) -Philippine Science High Schoo (PSHS) System recently concluded the three-day Visioning Workshop on the Futures of the PSHS System STEM Education Program held from 23 to 25 June 2026 at the PSHS-Main Campus in Diliman, Quezon City. Through futures thinking and strategic foresight methodologies, participants examined emerging trends, mapped systems and drivers of change, and developed future scenarios and action plans that could shape the next three decades of STEM education in Pisay. Conducted in partnership with the University of the Philippines National Institute for Science and Mathematics Education Development (UP NISMED) and the Department of Science and Technology – National Academy of Science and Technology, Philippines (DOST-NAST), the workshop was facilitated by Dr. Sheryl Lyn C. Monterola, Executive Director of UP NISMED, together with her team of experts in futures thinking and strategic foresight. DOST-NAST, led by Academician (Acd.) Jose Maria P. Balmaceda, likewise provided guidance and support throughout the workshop. The participants went through four key phases—Framing, Analysis, Creation, and Expression—culminating in the presentation of envisioned futures and strategic directions for the System. “Minsan marami tayong pina-plano pero kapag mayroon ng trahedyang nangyayari, hindi [na] tayo handa… [Gamitin] ang values, more than just reputation and competency,” Acd. Jose Maria P. Balmaceda said in his Closing Remarks. This activity forms part of the System’s efforts to develop a long-term foresight framework and an initial 30-year strategic horizon that will help guide policies, programs, and innovations as the country’s premier science high school system continues to nurture future scientists, innovators, and leaders for the nation. #HusayPisay #Pisay2056 #OneDOST4U

Next Batch of Pisay Scholars Successfully Wraps Up Their Science Immersion Journey in South Korea!

Five more scholars: Sheykhinah Hermionnie Valerie B. Valerio(PSHS-BRC), Thea Kathrine U. Galvez(PSHS-DRC), Doreen Sofia B. Kwon(PSHS-CVisCC) John Sebastian C. Solano(PSHS-SRC) , and Princess Jaclyn D. Plan(PSHS-CVC) have recently engaged in laboratory-intensive training conducted in Cardiovascular Metabolic Disease Core Research Support Center, Inje University on June 2-11, 2026, after the first batch of scholars participated SIP in the institution last May. This batch of students also immersed themselves in laboratory and research activities including Cell and Molecular Analysis, Cell Viability Assay, Animal Sacrifice, Echocardiography, and Confocal and Western Blot Imaging. These experiences enable them to practice and improve valuable research and laboratory skills under the mentorship of professional scientists and experts in the field of Research and Medical Science. Beyond laboratory skills, they also participated in collaborative discussions, culminating, and cultural activities, enriching their understanding and appreciation for both for the field of Science and the South Korean Culture. The Science Immersion Program is the PSHS System’s main tool to developing globally competitive Filipino scholars who are equipped with strong foundations in science and mathematics while also providing avenue to express their creativity, leadership, and entrepreneurial skills needed to address future challenges. The DOST-PSHS System once again extends its heartfelt gratitude to the researchers and mentors who generously shared their knowledge and expertise in their respective fields for the two batches of Pisay scholar-interns: Professor Jin Han, MD PhD, Professor Hyoung Kyu Kim, PhD; Professor Jae Beom Youm, MD, PhD; Maria Victoria Faith Garcia, PhD; Jubert Marquez, PhD; Jessa Flores, PhD Candidate; Prycelline Abedejos; and Jed Allyn Hernandez. Following this successful collaboration, the System looks forward to more promising scientific and educational partnerships that will open opportunities for future generations of Filipino students to engage in world-class research and global collaboration. #HusayPisay #ScienceImmersionProgram #PisaySIP #OneDOST4U
