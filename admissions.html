ADMISSIONS

Become a Pisay scholar. Admission to the Philippine Science High School System, including the Davao Region Campus, is through the National Competitive Examination (NCE). Scholars receive free tuition, a loan of textbooks, and a monthly stipend.

Who Can Apply

An applicant must:

  • Be a Filipino citizen with no pending or approved application as an immigrant to any foreign country
  • Be a Grade 6 pupil from a school recognized by the Department of Education
  • Have a final grade of at least 85% in Science and Mathematics, shown on the report card
  • Have a satisfactory rating in Character on the report card
  • Not have taken the PSHS National Competitive Examination before
  • Be in good health and fit for a rigorous academic program

Application Requirements

  • Fully accomplished application form
  • Recent 1x1 ID pictures (scanned copy for online applicants)
  • Copy of the latest report card
  • Processing fee: free for public school students and for private school students on full scholarship. Private school students pay a non-refundable fee. A certification of full scholarship from the school is required if applicable.

How to Apply

  1. Check that you meet the eligibility criteria above.
  2. Download and fill out the application form, or apply online at nce.pshs.edu.ph.
  3. Prepare the requirements and pay the fee if you are from a private school.
  4. Submit your application online, or in person at any PSHS campus or at a DOST Provincial Science and Technology Center.
  5. Receive your examination permit. If you have not received it two weeks before the exam, contact the nearest PSHS campus or DOST office.
  6. Take the National Competitive Examination on the assigned date and venue.

The Examination

The NCE is a scholastic aptitude test. It measures Scientific Ability, Quantitative Ability, Abstract Reasoning, and Verbal Aptitude. Only this one test is used for screening.

Selection of Qualifiers

Applicants are ranked by region based on their exam results. The top qualifiers in the regional ranking fill the campus quota. Qualifiers must complete the enrollment requirements, including medical and laboratory results, before admission is final.

Application Schedule

The application period for School Year 2027-2028 ran from April 6 to November 7, 2026. The schedule for the next cycle will be posted on this page and on the PSHS System website once announced.

Lateral Entry

Students who finished Grade 7 or Grade 8 outside the PSHS System may apply for lateral entry when slots are open. Applicants submit the required documents to the campus registrar and take a qualifying examination. Slots are limited and announced yearly.

Scholarship Privileges

  • Free tuition
  • Free loan of textbooks
  • Monthly stipend
  • Additional allowances for scholars from low income families

Inquiries

Visit the Registrar's Office at the Philippine Science High School Davao Region Campus, Sto. Niño, Tugbok District, Davao City. You may also call (082) 293 0002 to 04 or visit smc.pshs.edu.ph. See the Contact page for more details.
